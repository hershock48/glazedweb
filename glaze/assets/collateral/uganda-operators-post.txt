*Websites for lodges and safari operators, built to take bookings*

Lodges are giving Booking.com around 15% of every stay. Tour operators are
giving Viator and GetYourGuide 20-30% of every booking. Either way, the
platform owns your guest. A site that books directly keeps the margin, and
the guest, with you.

Glazed Web is a small U.S. web studio, on the ground in Entebbe with Andrew
Otage. Every site is custom designed for your lodge or safari company:
mobile-first, fast on local connections, and built so guests enquire, pay a
deposit, and book with you directly.

*How it works*
- The Baker's Dozen: US$1,900 once, plus US$450/yr care (hosting, security, edits)
- Online deposits through your own site: 1.5% of card deposits, charged only when you get paid
- First 90 days at 0%, so you watch it work before it costs a shilling
- Your photos, your story, your domain: you own the site, the code, and the accounts

We build a working preview of your own site first. You decide after you have
clicked through it. See our work: glazedweb.com
