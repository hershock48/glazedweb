## Divergences an owner would notice

Each of these is visible to a person moving between two of these screens.

1. **What the first screen is called.** The door opens on "Events" (copperac), "What’s on" (mikesplace), "Orders" (devine), "Leads" (anchor), "Counter" with a "Tickets (N)" tab (pjs), a "Kitchen" board opening on "86 Board" (cookinwithbeans), and "The Case" (scooplist). The same kitchen tab is "86 Board & Hours" in copperac and mikesplace, "86 Board" in cookinwithbeans and "Sold out and timing" in pjs, and in none of them does it hold opening hours.

2. **Where the save button sits and what it says.** Sticky at the bottom as "Save and publish" (copperac and mikesplace menus, anchor facts). At the top as "Save menu" beside "Compare latest saved menu" and "Download draft" (copperac kitchen editor). "Save menu" disabled until dirty with an "Unsaved changes" chip (cookinwithbeans). "Save the menu" at the bottom (pjs). "Save notes" (anchor lead) and "Save and add policies" (anchor book). Scooplist and devine's board save on the tap itself; scooplist shows a short toast, devine shows nothing on success.

3. **The vocabulary for "not available".** "Off the site" (copperac and mikesplace website menu, which leaves Toast untouched), "hidden" (the ordering documents in copperac, pjs and cookinwithbeans), "86" for tonight (four kitchens), "Sold out today" (pjs), "Tub's empty, take it off the board" or "86 it, it's done pouring" depending on trade (scooplist), "retired" (scooplist library), "Archive this event" (events). cookinwithbeans is the only one that explains the pair on the control itself: "Hidden from the order page (seasonal or discontinued; tonight-only is the 86 board)".

4. **What a failed save says.** "Your typing is still here." (copperac, mikesplace), "Your typing is still on screen." (anchor), "That did not save." (devine, pjs), "That didn't stick, try again." (scooplist), "The change was not confirmed. Refresh the board before trying again." (devine board), and nothing at all for a pjs ticket move or a cookinwithbeans 86 tap that returned an error.

5. **What a successful save says.** "Saved. Check the public menu to confirm how it looks." (copperac workroom), "Saved to the demo menu. Check the demo order page; cached pages may take a few seconds." (copperac kitchen editor), "Saved. The site shows it within a few seconds." (anchor), "Saved. Customers see it now." (pjs), "Saved.
