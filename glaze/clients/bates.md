## What they are

Two slider restaurants, family-owned since 1959. Livonia at 33406 5 Mile
Road, NW corner of 5 Mile and Farmington, (734) 427-3464, open Sun to Tue
9am to 10pm and Wed to Sat 7am to 10pm. Farmington Hills at 22291 Middlebelt
Road at 9 Mile, (248) 478-7350, 7am to 10pm daily. Burgers, breakfast,
coneys, chili, shakes. Carryout from a window at the back of the Livonia
building, names called on a speaker. Catering by phone; orders over 100
burgers need a deposit.

Noble and Laura Bates opened the first stand at Seven Mile and Beech Daly in
1955 and the Five Mile store on February 14, 1959. John and Barbara Bates ran
it from June 1962; Farmington Hills opened November 1976; John died in 2019.
Barbara owns both stores. Daughter Laurie Johnston was general manager on the
2020 About page; granddaughter Tara Smith is general manager in the April
2025 fire coverage. The Livonia store closed after a kitchen fire on April
29, 2025 and reopened; a blogger ordered online from it in October 2025.

Names: the site says Bates' Burgers, the POS says Bates Hamburgers, the
Instagram is `bates_hamburgers_livonia`, the Facebook page is
`batesburgersarebetter`.
