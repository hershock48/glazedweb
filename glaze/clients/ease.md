## What they are

Flat-pack corrugated trash and recycling boxes, made in the USA, sold from
810 Industrial Road, Marshall, Michigan 49068 (shipping and receiving at 428
South Linden), 269-420-0143. Started in 1995 inside the owners' party rental
company, to replace rentable trash cans; now sold wholesale to rental
companies, waste haulers, portable toilet companies, festivals and colleges
in the U.S., Canada and the Caribbean. Their own figures: over 2,000
customers, 78 percent reordering.

Owned by Jason and Katy DeVine, the family behind DeVine's Flowers and
Botanicals and Petals and Pour Coffee Shop (`devine.md`). The Contact page
also lists Kathy DeVine (accounting) and Max Hewitt (sales and marketing).

Three product lines: event boxes, 18 x 18 x 32, white or black, with or
without a lid, custom print on white; a 14.5 x 14.5 x 29.5 kraft box for
flower shops, caterers, coffee shops and salons; and a consumer kit for
empties and tailgates. Every box has the "Insta-Bottom", which opens without
tape, corner notches for the liner, and hand holes.

Names in use on the current site: EASE Containers (logo), TTC System (hero),
Party Time, Inc. (a testimonial), Supply Your Party (staff email, Instagram),
and the domain easedisposableboxes.com.

ground. Carton-paper ground, charcoal type, their logo green for actions,
kraft for labels, their logo navy for the footer. Headings in Oswald.

**"78% reorder" is said as "Nearly 8 in 10 customers order again."** Kevin,
2026-11-05: average consumers do not understand the stat. The tailgate page

**Order again is in scope.** Kevin, 2026-11-05: "maybe we could make it
easier to reorder." A button in every order email, a code on the carton
label, one reminder a year. Added to Exhibit A at the same price. Standing
orders are named as not included.
