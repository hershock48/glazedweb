## What they are

Brewery with taprooms including Birmingham. Core can lineup and a restaurant
operation.

## Why this file matters beyond the prospect

**This repo is the source of the proposal system.** Its stylesheet and animated
donut marks are lifted verbatim into every subsequent proposal rather than
re-derived. See `../proposal.md` for the six-section structure and the exact file
paths.

## Decisions on file

**Their incumbent contract keeps code ownership with the vendor.** Craftpeak,
February 2021: $13,500 upfront plus $350 a month, now billed by Arryved/Fullsteam
at $294 a month. **That contrast is what our terms sell against**, and it is the
single most useful fact in the proposal.

**They do have a current per-taproom tap list**, buried under Locations > taproom >
Beverages > Current Taplist. An earlier draft of the proposal claimed they had
none. That was wrong and was removed. Check a claim before it goes in a document
addressed to the owner.
