# Lemoncello Catering

**Repo** `donna` · **Live** lemoncello.glazedweb.com

## What they are

Home-cooked catering in Onaway, Michigan, Presque Isle County. Run by a member of
Kevin's family.

## Decisions on file

**Not a Next.js build.** A zero-dependency static site generator, with Resend for
the booking form. Do not "upgrade" it to the house stack without a reason.

**The brand assets are cut out of a single supplied logo image** by a Python tool
that re-cuts it into layers, so the mark can be animated. There is no vector
original.

**Copy voice:** "we" rather than "I", warm but plural. **Numbers she has not
supplied stay as visible placeholders rather than being invented.**

**Movement should be subtle and in the studio's own vocabulary**, taken from the
live Glazed Web and Chism sites rather than invented fresh.
