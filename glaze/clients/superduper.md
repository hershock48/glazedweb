**Neither build is live.** Checked 2026-09-17 through the Vercel API: both
projects are paused, carry no custom domain, and serve only `.vercel.app`
URLs. superduperdrinks.com is not served by either, so it is the client's own

## What they are

Coffee, retail and wholesale. Retail locations Kevin knows: Horrocks in Battle
Creek (new, similar to the Meijer rollout), Flatiron Brewing, and the Slow Roll in
Detroit.

## Decisions on file

**The client does not want the brand changed.** Keep the existing logo, the teal
and yellow palette and the product photography; improve the website, the copy and
the UX only. This is the play that got repeated on Copper AC and Cascarelli's.

**They want "adventure" positioning.** That is the client's word and it is the
brief for the copy voice.

**Kevin's favourite line from the pitch copy:** "Coffee that shows up before you
run out."

Fifteen product pages plus SEO routes.
